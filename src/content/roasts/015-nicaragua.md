playbook: |-
  ## SETUP

  1. Chaff tray empty. 227 g green in drum.
  2. **Hot charge preheat** to 230–250°F B-temp: press **½ lb + any P + START**, drum OUT, run **1:30**, press **OFF**. Check B-temp. If under 230°F, run another 1:30 cycle. Don't exceed 1:30 in a single cycle.
  3. Drum in. Chaff tray in. Door closed.

  ## ROAST

  4. Press **½ lb + P1 + START**. *Immediately* press **P5** (Manual, 100%). Start phone timer.
  5. **Log B-temp every 30 sec** in interventions.
  6. **B-temp crosses 300°F:** press **P4 (75%)**. Press **D** immediately after — prevents stall when power drops.
  7. **B-temp falls under 290°F:** press **P5** to bring it back up. Step P5↔P4 to hold ~290–310°F.
  8. **Dry end (~4–5 min):** yellow color, hay → bread.
  9. **~5–7 min: scattered isolated pops = false crack.** Ignore. Keep band-riding.
  10. **75% Err 7 shutoff at 9:00:** press **START** to continue.
  11. **Real FC (~8–10 min):** sustained rhythmic popping. Mark time. Switch to **P3 (50%)**.
  12. **Clock running low pre-2C:** press **C** to extend.
  13. **First 1–3 snaps of audible 2C → press Cool within 10 sec.** Forward-think the press by ~15s (cool-cycle carryover).

  ## EXPECTED TIMING (hot charge)

  | Sound | Time |
  | --- | --- |
  | False crack (scattered pops) | ~5–7 min |
  | Real FC (sustained roll) | ~8–10 min |
  | Real 2C (faster sharper crackles) | ~10–12 min |

  Identifying sounds: [FC vs 2C](/roasts/behmor-2000ab/#fc-vs-2c--sound). Color and smell: [progressions](/roasts/behmor-2000ab/#sensory-cues--sound-color-smell).

  ## SPECIFIC RISK THIS RUN

  - First time on this bean — FC may arrive earlier or later than the Guatemala. Don't drop on the clock; drop on the **first snaps of real 2C**.
  - B-temp pinned at 310–314°F = the [element self-limiter](/roasts/behmor-2000ab/#element-self-limiting-310314-f-b-temp) has kicked in. Drop to P4 or P3 to take ramp control back.

  General safety (bluish smoke, past-2C, etc.): [critical safety constraints](/roasts/behmor-2000ab/#critical-safety-constraints).
